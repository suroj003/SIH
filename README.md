# 🏞️ LandSetu — National Land Acquisition & Management System

> A digital platform for transparent, efficient and accountable land acquisition management.

LandSetu is a web-based Land Acquisition Management System designed to digitize and streamline the land acquisition lifecycle — from land identification and project initiation to verification, compensation, grievance handling and final acquisition.

The platform brings citizens, land officers and administrators onto a common system with role-based access, centralized land records, acquisition case tracking, compensation monitoring, document management and project-level analytics.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Land acquisition in India involves multiple stakeholders including citizens, landowners, project agencies, district authorities, state governments and central departments.

Traditional workflows often depend on:

- Fragmented records
- Manual documentation
- Paper-based approvals
- Limited inter-department coordination
- Difficult case tracking
- Delayed compensation processing
- Lack of centralized monitoring
- Limited transparency for citizens

LandSetu aims to address these issues through a centralized digital workflow and real-time information system.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

LandSetu provides a unified platform where the complete acquisition process can be monitored digitally.
